Common Operating Environment — approval channel

Whether it is a plant floor or a back-of-house, the person who owns the stock is not at a screen. They do not open dashboards — which is the real failure mode of this category, not features. So CrossHaul calls them, states the decision it already made, and takes "yes" for an answer.

Why the call says "units", not a commodity. The primitive is sector-independent: it holds wherever multiple sites sit under one owner, stock dates, site demand varies independently, and a transfer costs less than a purchase. Food service is the vertical deployed, not the ceiling. The transfer above is chosen by an execution-aware decision — vehicles, distance and spare capacity, not quantities alone. That is where the physical AI sits: as intra-city autonomy takes a 10-unit leg from ~$7 to ~$2, more transfers turn positive. Beneficiary of that autonomy, not a producer of it.

Two paths, deliberately. The recorded clip plays from disk — identical every rehearsal and immune to venue wifi, which is what the stage needs. The live agent is what the judges' table needs: ten concurrent calls, so several people can interrogate it at once.

How it is wired. The agent is published with authentication off and this domain on its allowlist, with Fail when Origin header is missing enabled — so it answers this page and refuses a bare curl. No API key ever reaches the browser; the agent id is public by design, and the worst an abuser gets is our own call minutes.
