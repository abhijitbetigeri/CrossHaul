Go to market

Sell them the proof before the product

The wedge

A paid waste audit

Read-only, three weeks, from their own invoices. It returns a dated, priced ledger of the transfers that should have happened. No new engineering — our pipeline, pointed backwards.

The land

A cluster, never one site

One site has no counterparty, so there is no single-location pilot. Minimum deployment is three sites — $8,964 of ARR on day one, and a constraint the incumbents share.

The motion

Founder-led, drivable

Bay Area clusters, afternoon walk-ins, then restaurant-specialist accountants on referral. 17 groups in year one — one signature every three weeks.

And the thing that would kill it: if the median audit comes back under $8,000 per location per year, the return does not clear the price and nothing else on these slides matters. Ten audits answer that by day 90.

The audit is the strongest idea in the deck: it is the sales motion and the customer discovery and the cheapest test of our riskiest assumption, all at once. Say the kill criterion out loud — volunteering it is what makes the rest credible.
