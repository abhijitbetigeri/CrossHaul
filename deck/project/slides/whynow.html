Why this was not built five years ago

Coordination got cheap

Per-site agents negotiating continuously only became affordable in the last eighteen months. Before that, cross-site planning meant an enterprise ERP that only fifty-unit chains could buy.

Margins stopped forgiving waste

Food and labour costs since 2024 mean operators will now pay for single-digit-percent recovery they used to write off without looking.

Operators are crossing into multi-site

3,381 locations are moving through the one-to-nineteen unit transitions in 2026 alone. The day they open a third site, they acquire a problem they have never had.

And nobody sells them a tool for it — which is the next slide.
