Solo, and specific about it

Sixteen products shipped in four months. This one already won a hackathon in July — you are not being asked to believe a solo founder could build it.

I am not a restaurant person. I am a distributed-systems person, and a group whose twelve sites cannot see each other's inventory has a coordination problem, not a cooking problem.

I know what I am missing. Both are hires, both are dated, both are already in the model.

Hire 1 · month 3

Founding engineer, forecasting

Owns ingestion from messy POS and invoice data — the dependency every other claim rests on.

Hire 2 · month 13

Mid-market account executive

Only after founder-led selling has closed year one. Hiring a seller before that hides the problem instead of fixing it.

What I have never done: run a restaurant, carry a quota, or manage anyone. The first hire is the test of the last one.

Do not apologise for being solo. Founder Institute admits individual founders and one of the judges runs two of its chapters. The last line — naming what you have never done — is the one that earns the slide.
