The primitive

One picture. One decision.

01

Forecast per site

Demand per menu item, exploded through the recipe bill-of-materials into ingredient requirements.

02

Net against sister sites

A shortage checks every other site for surplus — nearest first, nearest-expiry first — before it checks a supplier.

03

Buy only the remainder

Only the net group shortage reaches a supplier — as one purchase order, for one human approval.

Everything sector-specific — recipes, production schedules, category hierarchies — sits on top of that. The primitive does not care what is in the crate.

Three beats, one sentence each. Do not read the cards. The line that lands is the last one: the primitive does not care what is in the crate.
